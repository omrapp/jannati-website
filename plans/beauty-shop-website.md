# Jannati Beauty Shop Website

***Goal*** isto build an ecommerce website for Jannati Beauty Shop (The World Of Beauty In One Place), 
for selling beauty producs for men and women in Arabic gulf countries based in Muscat, Oman.


### Description

`Jannati` is a beauty shop for selling beauty products in Muscat Oman, and We want to build a website exactly like https://darbeauty.com/
for the shop to sell product online, Move the shop to digital channel.

The shop have experience over 15 years in beauty products.

I already downloaded the https://darbeauty.com/ website for reference and to fetch what we need to achieve the goal for building our website.

Directory: @darbeauty.com/


### Features

- Login/Logout via email and social media account like Google and Facebook
- Categorize and organize products by categories and brands 
- Implement Cart and orders 
- Apply payment via Credit Card like MasterCard, Visa also American Express also Oman local payment methods if available
- Able to track orders
- Full Admin panel to control the website contents and products (CRUD)
- Support Arabic/English language (Arabic is the default)
- Page for New products and most selling 
- Support Search, Sorting and filtering.
- Support Dark and Light Themes
